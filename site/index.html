Set up once. The next form is one click.

College applications, KYC, and job forms ask for the same name, the same marks, and the same address. SlothFill reads your PDFs on this computer and fills the fields. The documents never leave the machine.

  3. FillOne click on a form. SlothFill does not submit it.
