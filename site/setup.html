Read your PDFs once

Gemma reads an ID, a marksheet, or a resume on this computer and drafts a profile. You check it before any form is filled. The file is not uploaded anywhere.

A text PDF is read as text. A scan is rendered and sent to Gemma vision. On CPU this can take a few minutes, and it happens once.

Or read a folder on this computer

Lists the PDFs in that folder only. Subfolders stay put. Uncheck anything that should not become part of the profile, then read. Up to 20 files.
